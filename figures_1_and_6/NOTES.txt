REDESIGNED FIGURES 1 AND 6

Open figures_1_and_6.drawio in diagrams.net / draw.io. It contains two pages.
Individual .drawio, .pdf, .svg and .png files are also provided.
PDF/SVG figures are 180 mm wide. PNG exports are 450 dpi at that width.
Analytical axes, labels and bars are vector objects in the full PDF/SVG.
The HTML files are self-contained, offline previews.

Source material
- lcz_paper_v5.docx, including Figure 1/6 captions and Online Methods.
- Original figures: lcz_paper_v5/word/media/image1.png and image6.png.
- Chen, Li and Liu (2022), Scientific Data 9:125,
  doi:10.1038/s41597-022-01208-6, particularly Figure 1 and pp. 2-5.

Design
The scientific-figure-making skill supplies the Arial typography, white
background, restrained palette, editable vector text, hatching and print sizing.
The diagram-design skill supplies the tinted stage zones, semantic shape/border
types, orthogonal connections and separation of demand from spatial allocation.
The explicit request for draw.io determines the editable authoring format.

Figure 1
- Preserves the original static/SSP1/SSP3/SSP5 maps, WRF illustration,
  static temperature map, spatial bias, humidex curves and decomposition.
- Makes the paired CASPER comparison explicit: the city, decade, atmospheric
  predictors, RCP and terrain match; the LCZ-derived land-use input changes.
- Redraws decomposition from original rounded labels: 0.58, 0.38, 0.14 K,
  and 15%, 15%, 7% intensification. Segment widths are approximate because
  they are calculated from those rounded labels, not raw analysis output.
- Humidex curve pixels are preserved from the original figure, with newly
  drawn axes, scenario legends and titles. The axes are calibrated to the
  original pixel coordinates. No time-series values were invented/digitized.
- The spatial bias and static temperature images have no original colorbars;
  they are treated as qualitative illustrations. No scale was invented.

Figure 6
- Preserves all five numbered processes and the main input/output relations.
- Replaces the tiny fraction-chart illustration with its explicit product label.
- Historical LCZ maps feed both Markov analysis and LEAS. Markov products
  inform LCZ demand and the transition matrix. Expansion maps plus driving
  factors train the random forest; the spatial allocator receives probabilities,
  demand and the transition matrix. The projected maps connect to CASPER.
- Keeps the original WUDAPT-to-WRF (w2w) conversion stage.
- Distinguishes raw 1-km climate fields from derived UHI/humidex diagnostics.

Editability and verification
Boxes, labels, connections, stage boundaries and LCZ swatches are native
draw.io objects. Maps and the WRF illustration remain embedded PNGs; analytical
panels are embedded SVG objects. Panel text is editable in the standalone SVG
or can be regenerated with the Python script. Individual map cells and curve
values are not editable data series in draw.io.

The Windows Electron exporter could not start in the sandbox. PDF/PNG/SVG
exports therefore use Matplotlib to render the same native node geometry and
explicit connection routes. XML/image integrity, required graph paths, bounds,
offline HTML checks and rendered PDFs were checked. Opening and re-exporting
inside the draw.io desktop app was not tested; minor text wrapping may differ.

Scientific scope
The reference paper uses FLUS and PFT products; this study uses PLUS and LCZs.
Only its stage-layout grammar is borrowed. Its eight SSP-RCP source scenarios
differ from the manuscript's eight climate pairings. The manuscript pairings
are preserved; the mapping from source land scenarios needs author verification
and cannot be resolved from the supplied figure images.
No model results were recomputed. The manuscript and existing v1 exports remain.

Rebuild from the workspace root
  python -B figures_redesign/scripts/redesign_figures.py
  python -B figures_redesign/scripts/check_redesign.py
Requires existing matplotlib, Pillow and Node.js with the bundled pdf-lib;
no new dependency was installed. vector_panels.mjs points to this machine's
existing runtime dependency directory.
