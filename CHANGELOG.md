## 1.0.0

First release. This version produced every figure, table and number in
*Dynamic urban morphology projections reveal critical biases in future climate
risk assessment*.

- Stage 1 projects LCZ composition to 2100 under five pathways.
- Stage 2 automates CASPER downscaling across 264 scenario runs.
- Quick-start example runs the whole of stage 1 on synthetic inputs in under 10 seconds.
- Regression tests compare the example against stored output.
